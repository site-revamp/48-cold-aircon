# 48 COLD Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

48 COLD PTE. LTD. provides aircon servicing, chemical washing, installation, repairs, and VRV project services for homes and businesses in Singapore. The proposed homepage keeps the “SIBEH COLD” brand personality while helping visitors understand service timelines, compare plans, and request the right service.

## Main Improvements

1. **Clarify the two-day promise.** The original homepage leaves it unclear whether two days means an appointment, diagnosis, or completed repair. If 48 COLD can reliably meet the timeframe, the redesign would explain what it covers, where it applies, and how parts availability or delays affect it. A clear booking process and examples of completed work would support the message.
2. **Make servicing prices easier to compare.** The original table leaves per-visit costs and some contract conditions unclear. Separate cards for one-time servicing and annual contracts would show visit counts, total and per-visit costs, inclusions, and GST exclusions. Customers would have a direct way to request a quote for work that requires inspection.
3. **Give customers a direct route from problem to enquiry.** Repeated service information makes it harder to find the next step. The redesign would group common issues such as leaks and weak cooling with relevant enquiry options, give BCA registration and completed work more visibility, and use confirmed public phone and WhatsApp details.

## Design Improvements

Distinct pricing cards would separate one-time visits from three- and four-visit contracts. Grouping common faults with the corresponding service and enquiry action would make the page easier to scan.
